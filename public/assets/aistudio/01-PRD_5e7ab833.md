# PRD — Product Requirements Document
## NutriWise USA — Health & Nutrition Platform

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** October 2026  
**Status:** Approved for Development

---

## 1. Project Overview

### 1.1 Purpose
NutriWise USA is a comprehensive health and nutrition web platform targeting United States adults and families. The platform delivers evidence-based nutrition content, AI-powered meal planning, personalized diet guidance, and professional dietitian services — all in one place.

### 1.2 Mission Statement
Make healthy eating simple, personalized, and practical for every American household.

### 1.3 Core Promise
- Trusted, evidence-based nutrition content
- AI-powered personalized meal plans
- Practical tools for real-life grocery shopping and meal prep
- Professional dietitian access when needed

## 2. Target Users

### 2.1 Primary Audience

## 4. Core Features & Functionality

### 4.1 Phase 1 — Content & Foundation

#### Health & Wellness Blog
- Articles covering: sleep, hydration, stress, heart health, digestion, energy
- Each article must include: author, professional reviewer, publication date, last-reviewed date, references
- Categories: Weight Management | Heart Health | Digestive Wellness | Energy | Sleep & Recovery | Hydration
- Tag system for cross-linking content
- Reading time estimate per article

#### Healthy Foods Database
- Individual page per food item
- Data fields per food:
  - Food name, category, image
  - Serving size (US household measures + grams)
  - Calories, protein, total carbs, fiber, sugar, total fat, saturated fat, sodium
  - Key vitamins and minerals
  - Preparation methods
  - Storage instructions
  - Common allergens
  - Related recipes
  - Food comparisons
- Data source: USDA FoodData Central API (free, public)
- Search and filter by category, nutrient, dietary preference

#### Recipe Library
- Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks, Meal Prep categories
- Each recipe page includes:
  - Total prep time, cook time, servings
  - Ingredient list with US measurements
  - Step-by-step instructions
  - Estimated nutrition per serving (calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber)
  - Common allergens
  - Ingredient substitutions
  - Storage and leftover guidance
  - Related recipes
  - User ratings and reviews
- Filters: Budget | Time | Dietary preference | Ingredients | Meal type
- Serving size calculator (1, 2, 4, 6 people)
- Print/export recipe card

#### Diet Plans & Meal Planning Library
- Pre-built plan types: Balanced Eating | Weight Management | High-Protein | Mediterranean | Vegetarian | Family Meal Planning
- Each plan includes a 7-day sample calendar with breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks
- Linked recipes for each meal slot
- Downloadable weekly plan PDF

#### Search
- Global search across: articles, foods, recipes, diet plans
- Auto-suggest as user types
- Filter results by content type

### 4.2 Phase 2 — AI Nutrition Tools

#### AI Feature 1: Personalized Meal Plan Generator
**Onboarding questionnaire:**
1. Goal (general healthy eating / weight management / muscle nutrition / family meal planning)
2. Age range and activity level
3. Dietary preferences (vegetarian / vegan / pescatarian / Mediterranean / no restriction)
4. Food allergies and intolerances
5. Daily food budget ($25–$200/week)
6. Cooking time available per day
7. Cooking skill level (beginner / intermediate / advanced)
8. Favorite and disliked foods
9. Meals per day and household size

**AI Output:**
- 7-day meal calendar (Mon–Sun)
- Breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks per day
- Recipe details for each meal
- Estimated nutrition per day (calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber)
- Ingredient substitutions
- Combined weekly grocery list
- "Replace this meal" button per meal slot
- Plan adjustment based on user feedback

**Important Disclaimer:** AI-generated plans are general wellness guidance only. Not for medical conditions, pregnancy, minors, eating disorders, or medically restricted diets.

#### AI Feature 2: AI Nutrition Chatbot
- Natural language Q&A on food and nutrition topics
- Personalized food recommendations
- Recipe suggestions
- Meal plan editing via chat
- Follow-up question handling
- Links to relevant articles and food pages
- Saves user food preferences (with explicit permission)
- Escalates medical questions to recommend professional consultation
- Health claims backed by cited sources

**Example queries:**
- "Give me healthy breakfast ideas under $5"
- "High-fiber vegetarian lunch recipes"
- "How to prepare healthier meals with chicken and rice"
- "Create a grocery list for 5 dinners"

#### AI Feature 3: Smart Recipe Generator
- User inputs available ingredients
- AI suggests matching recipes
- Output: title, prep time, servings, ingredients, steps, estimated nutrition, allergen info, storage guidance
- Filters: Under 20 min | Budget-friendly | High-fiber | Vegetarian | Dairy-free | Meal-prep friendly | Family-friendly
- Allergy substitutions are suggestions only — AI does not guarantee safety

#### AI Feature 4: Smart Grocery Planner
- Auto-generates shopping list from meal plan
- Combines duplicate ingredients
- Budget-based substitutions
- Pantry exclusion (user marks what they already have)
- Organized by store section: Produce | Dairy | Meat | Pantry | Frozen
- Print and export to PDF
- Household serving adjustment

### 4.3 Phase 3 — Premium Tools

#### AI Feature 5: Food Photo Analyzer
- User uploads meal photo
- AI identifies foods and estimates ingredients
- Approximate calories and macros displayed as a range
- Healthier preparation suggestions
- Save to food diary option
- All estimates labeled as approximate — user can manually edit

#### AI Feature 6: Food Label Scanner
- User uploads Nutrition Facts label image
- AI explains: serving size, servings per package, calories, protein, carbs, fiber, sugars, saturated fat, sodium
- Ingredient list plain-language summary
- Comparison with similar products
- Alternative product suggestions based on user preferences

#### AI Feature 7: AI Progress Coach
- Weekly meal consistency summary
- Estimated fiber and protein intake trends
- Meal prep reminders (email/push)
- Weekly habit summary
- Goal-related educational suggestions
- Plan optimization recommendations
- Focus: habit consistency, food variety, balanced eating (not only weight/calories)

#### Food Diary / Progress Tracker
- Manual food logging
- Photo logging (via AI Photo Analyzer)
- Daily nutrition summary
- Weekly progress charts
- Exportable reports

### 4.4 Phase 4 — Professional Services

#### Dietitian / Nutritionist Directory
- Professional profiles: name, photo, credentials, specializations, bio
- RD (Registered Dietitian) credential clearly displayed
- Online booking calendar
- Consultation payment (Stripe)
- Appointment reminders (email)
- Secure client intake forms
- Professional-reviewed meal plans
- Follow-up appointments
- Clear distinction: AI plans vs. professionally reviewed plans

## 7. Non-Functional Requirements

| Category | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Performance | Page load under 2 seconds on 4G mobile |

| Language | English (US) only |
| Browser Support | Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge — last 2 major versions |

## 9. Compliance & Safety Requirements

- **Medical Disclaimer:** Prominent on all AI tools and health content pages. Clear statement that content is general wellness guidance, not medical advice.
- **FTC Compliance:** All health benefit claims must be truthful, non-misleading, and evidence-based. Affiliate relationships clearly disclosed.
- **FDA Boundary:** AI features limited to general wellness guidance. No disease diagnosis, prevention, or treatment claims.
- **Privacy:** Data minimization, explicit consent before collecting health-related data, encryption, user deletion rights.
- **HIPAA Note:** Platform is not a HIPAA-covered entity, but health data privacy is treated as a core product value. Legal review required before launch.
- **AI Guardrails:** Emergency symptoms, complex medical conditions, medication interactions → AI escalates to professional consultation recommendation.
- **Sensitive Users:** Minors, pregnancy, serious allergies, eating disorders → additional warnings and professional referral.
- **Content Quality:** No AI-auto-published health content. All articles require human review by qualified author or reviewer.
